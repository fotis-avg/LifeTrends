LifeTrends Privacy Policy

LifeTrends runs entirely on your device. It reads Health data (steps, walking/running distance, flights climbed, exercise minutes, resting heart rate, heart-rate variability, respiratory rate, blood oxygen, active energy, dietary energy, body weight, body fat percentage, time in daylight, sleep) and photo metadata (creation date and GPS coordinate only) solely to compute trends shown to you in the app. This data is never uploaded, shared, sold, or stored outside your device. The app contains no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and makes no network connections. Revoking Health or Photos permission in iOS Settings stops all access immediately.
